How does location sharing work?

Pinny shares your location with your family only when you open the app, tap Refresh or Check in, or send an SOS. It never tracks you in the background. Each pin shows how long ago that person last shared.

Why is someone's location old?

Their pin updates only when they open Pinny. Ask them to open the app or tap Refresh.

How do I add my family?

Create a family, then share its invite code from the Family tab. Each person installs Pinny, signs in, and enters the code to join.

What are saved places?

Anyone in the family can save up to 10 places, such as Home or School. When someone shares their location inside a saved place, the family sees "At Home" instead of a street name. Saved places are visible to your family only.

How do I stop notifications?

Turn off check-in notifications in Pinny's Settings, or turn off all Pinny notifications in the iOS Settings app.

How do I leave a family?

Leave from the Family tab. From that moment your family can't see your location and you can't see theirs.

How do I delete my account?

In Pinny, go to Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account and location data straight away. See the privacy policy for details.

I was invited to test Pinny. How do I install it?

Install Apple's free TestFlight app from the App Store, then open the invite email on your iPhone and tap the link. Pinny appears on your home screen. Test builds expire after 90 days; TestFlight tells you when a new one is ready.
