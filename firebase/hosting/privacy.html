Pinny Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Pinny is a location-sharing app for your family only. Your location is shared with your family only when you open Pinny, tap Refresh or Check in, or send an SOS. There is no background tracking and no location history. We don't show ads, we don't track you across other apps, and we don't sell your data. Pinny is offered worldwide, and your data is stored in Sydney, Australia.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, your email address (from email sign-in, Sign in with Google, or Sign in with Apple, which may be a private relay address), a user ID, and a profile photo if you set one (from your Google account, or one picture you choose from your photo library; Pinny never reads the rest of your library).
  • Location: your most recently shared location (coordinates and how accurate they are), the time it was shared, and what triggered it (opening the app, Refresh or Check in, or SOS).
  • Battery: your battery level and whether your phone is charging, captured at the same moment as your location.
  • Family: your family's name, its invite code and its list of members.
  • Saved places: places your family adds, such as Home or School: a name, an icon, a centre point and a radius.
  • Chat: messages you send in your family chat, including SOS messages.
  • Notifications: a push notification token for your phone, so Pinny can send you notifications.
  • Family Pass purchase: if you buy the Family Pass, Apple handles the payment. Pinny never sees your card, Apple Pay or Apple ID details. We store only Apple's transaction identifier for the purchase, tied to your account, so that your pass can be restored and can't be used by a second account.

We don't collect your contacts, your photo library (only the one picture you choose as your profile photo), advertising identifier or browsing activity, and Pinny contains no analytics or advertising tools.

When your location is collected

Only in the moment you open Pinny (or bring it back on screen), tap Refresh or Check in, or send an SOS. Pinny asks for location access only "While Using the App". It never collects your location in the background, and saved places are only used to label a location you have already shared. Nothing is monitored.

Who can see it

  • Your family only. Your location, battery, saved places and chat are visible only to members of your family, and our database security rules enforce this. Saved places are visible to your family only.
  • When you leave a family, your family can no longer see your location from that moment, and you can no longer see theirs.
  • Profile photo: stored in Sydney, Australia, and visible to signed-in Pinny users, so your family sees your face on your pin. It is used for nothing else, and you can remove it at any time in Settings.
  • We don't share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Services that process your data

  • Google Firebase stores the data in Sydney, Australia (wherever in the world you use Pinny), handles sign-in (including Sign in with Google), runs the notification and purchase-verification logic and sends push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Apple delivers push notifications, provides Sign in with Apple and processes the Family Pass purchase through the App Store under Apple's own privacy policy. Apple tells us if a purchase is refunded, so we can remove the pass. Street names shown under a family member's pin are looked up on your phone using Apple's maps service, which sends the coordinates to Apple. Place search text is sent to Apple to find addresses. We don't store street addresses or your searches.
  • Notifications can include a family member's name and the name of a saved place (for example "At Home."), but never coordinates or a map link. They pass through Google and Apple to reach your family's phones but are not stored by us.

How long we keep it

  • We keep only your latest location. Each new share replaces the previous one. There is no history, and we don't keep backup copies of your location.
  • Your account data, including your profile photo, is kept until you remove it or delete your account.
  • The Family Pass transaction identifier is kept while the pass is active. It is removed if Apple refunds the purchase, and deleted with your account. Because the purchase belongs to your Apple ID, you can restore it in a new Pinny account.
  • Chat messages and saved places belong to the family. They stay when a member leaves or deletes their account, and they are deleted when the last member leaves the family.
  • To show the map quickly, Pinny keeps a copy of your family's latest locations on your phone until newer ones replace them or you delete the app.

Deleting your account

Delete your account at any time in Pinny: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account, your profile photo and your location data, including battery and accuracy, and removes you from your family. Messages you sent and places you saved stay with your family, as explained above.

Your choices

  • Turn off location access for Pinny in the iOS Settings app at any time.
  • Turn off check-in notifications in Pinny's Settings, or all notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Leave your family at any time.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored by Google Firebase, which encrypts stored data. Access is limited to you and your family by our security rules.

Children

Pinny is designed for families. If a child uses Pinny, a parent or guardian should set it up and supervise its use.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Pinny handles your data, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Where your data is stored and your rights

Pinny is available worldwide. Wherever you are, your data is stored and processed in Sydney, Australia, by Google Firebase on our behalf, and handled under the Australian Privacy Principles. If you are in the EU, UK or another place with its own privacy law, you have the right to access, correct and delete your data: deletion is built into the app (Settings → Delete account), and for anything else email us below.
